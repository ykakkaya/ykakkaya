### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a developer based in **Türkiye** who builds end-to-end products — from **Laravel** back-ends and APIs to cross-platform **Flutter** mobile apps — and take them all the way to production.

- 💳 Author of **[YkPos](https://github.com/ykakkaya/ykpos)** — a single Laravel package for **38 Turkish virtual POS** providers (19 banks + 19 payment institutions), with 3D Secure, refunds and installment queries
- 📱 Building Flutter apps for e-commerce, field operations and personal productivity
- 🛒 Delivering WordPress / WooCommerce and custom Laravel solutions for businesses
- 🎓 **Backend + Flutter workshop** at İstanbul Aydın University, Oct 2026 ([source code](https://github.com/ykakkaya/ia--flutter-workshop))
- 🎥 Sharing Python, PHP and Flutter tutorials on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@ykakkaya)
- 📫 Open to collaboration and freelance projects — reach me via [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/ykakkaya) or [ykakkaya.com](https://ykakkaya.com)
